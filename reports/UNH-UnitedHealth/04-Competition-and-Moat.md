# 4. Competition and Moat | UNH

## 4.1 Industry structure

US health benefits remain an **oligopoly of nationals** (UNH, Elevance, CVS/Aetna, Cigna, Humana in MA) plus Blues and regionals. Pharmacy benefit management is concentrated (Optum Rx, CVS Caremark, Express Scripts/Cigna). Vertical integration (payer + care + PBM) is UNH’s distinctive configuration—and a regulatory target.

## 4.2 Porter five forces (brief)

| Force | Assessment |
|-------|------------|
| Rivalry | High on MA bids, employer RFPs, Medicaid |
| Buyer power | High (CMS, large employers, state Medicaid) |
| Supplier power | Rising (hospitals/physicians via NSA IDR; pharma) |
| New entrants | Low at national scale; fintech/care startups chip niches |
| Substitutes | Public programs, self-insured + TPA, direct primary care |

## 4.3 Moat table (must be evidenced)

| Moat source | Evidence | Rating | Thinning path |
|-------------|----------|--------|---------------|
| Scale / data | ~48M UHC members; Optum >120M consumers touched | **Wide** | Membership loss; data-use restrictions |
| Vertical integration | Optum Health + Insight + Rx + UHC | **Wide but contested** | Antitrust / DOJ; forced separation risk |
| Switching costs | Employer contracts; provider networks | **Medium-wide** | Employer attrition (−165k Q3) |
| Brand / trust | Historically strong; damaged 2025–26 | **Medium** | Legal headlines, prior-auth politics |
| Cost advantage | Claims scale + Optum medical management | **Medium-wide** | NSA IDR (~100bps+ cost headwind per CFO) |

## 4.4 Peer cross-check (qualitative + multiples)

| Peer | Role | Relative read |
|------|------|---------------|
| ELV | Closest diversified benefits peer | Often more “pure” benefits; UNH Optum adds complexity/premium |
| HUM | MA specialist | Higher MA beta; UNH broader |
| CI / CVS | PBM + insurance mixes | PBM reform hits all; Optum Rx transparency push is industry signaling |

UNH TTM operating margin ~**5.9%**, ROE ~**16%**—below its own historical peak ROE years, consistent with mid-repair.

**Verdict:** Moat remains **wide on scale and integration**, but **trust and regulatory forces are actively testing it**. Do not write “impregnable moat.”
