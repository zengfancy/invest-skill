# 2. Fundamentals | UnitedHealth Group (UNH)

## 2.1 How the business makes money

UnitedHealth Group runs two complementary engines:

1. **UnitedHealthcare (UHC)** — risk-based and fee-based health benefits for employers, Medicare Advantage / duals, Medicaid (Community & State), and individuals. Profit = premiums − medical costs − operating costs (underwriting cycle).  
2. **Optum** — care delivery and value-based care (**Optum Health**), tech/admin/analytics (**Optum Insight**), and pharmacy care (**Optum Rx**). Large internal revenue with UHC is eliminated in consolidation.

Cash quality: insurance float + Optum operating cash; medical reserves and government payment timing swing quarterly CFO.

## 2.4 Strengths / risks

- **Strengths:** Scale in benefits + vertically integrated Optum; pricing power returning; FCF and capital return intact; Insight margins strong.  
- **Risks:** Medical cost trend still “elevated vs history”; MA membership and Stars; Optum Health shrinking VBC footprint; legal/regulatory overhang; GAAP vs adjusted optics.

**Bottom line:** Earnings power is recovering **via pricing, mix, and cost management while shrinking lives**—classic insurer repair, not volume growth.
