# 3. Recent Structural Changes (1–2 Years) | UNH

## 3.1 Timeline

| Date | Event | Evidence | Cycle vs structure |
|------|-------|----------|--------------------|
| 2024–2025 | Medical cost shock (MA intensity, NSA/IDR) | Industry-wide MCR spike; UNH multiple compression | **Cycle + structure** |
| 2025-05 | Reported DOJ criminal probe (Medicare) | Large drawdown; overhang persists into 2026 | **Structure (legal)** |
| 2025–2026 | Leadership / governance refresh | New Public Responsibility Committee; Hemsley CEO narrative | **Structure** |
| 2026-01 | Optum realignment (e.g., Optum Financial → Insight) | 10-Q disclosure; prior periods recast | **Structure** |
| 2026 H1–Q3 | Deliberate membership pruning + reprice | MA −935k YTD; employer attrition; margins up YoY | **Structure** |
| 2026-07 / 10 | Guidance raises (adj EPS) | Q2 then Q3 lift to **≥$20.50** adj | Repair confirmation |
| 2026-10 | Stars / state AG headlines | 2027 MA Stars commentary; Texas AG probe notes | **Structure risk** |

## 3.2 Cycle vs structure

| Cycle | Seasonal claims, prior-period reserve development, near-term medical trend |
|-------|------|
| Structure | Smaller, higher-quality book; Optum Health exits weak VBC markets; PBM transparency model; prior-auth reform; legal/governance reset |

## 3.3 Checklist

| Item | Status |
|------|--------|
| Pricing / benefits redesign | In progress; margins expanding YoY |
| Membership strategy | Shrink to improve economics |
| Optum Health | Fewer VBC patients; better margin on remaining book |
| Capital return | Buybacks ≥$5B FY26 target; dividends ongoing |
| Regulatory | DOJ overhang; Stars economics; Medicaid redeterminations |

## 3.4 Conclusion

UNH shifted from “grow lives at any cost” to **“earn the right margin on fewer lives + Optum discipline.”** That is structural. Whether the stock re-rates to old multiples depends on **clean MCR**, **MA Stars**, and **legal clarity**—not just one beat-and-raise quarter.
