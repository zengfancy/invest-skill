## One-line match

Underwriting and Optum margins are healing after the medical-cost shock, with **FY26 adjusted EPS guided to ≥$20.50**; at **~$417 / $374B** market cap, forward PE ~**18** and FCF yield ~**6%** look closer to a **repairing franchise** than a broken one—but **Medicare Advantage membership loss, Stars, and DOJ overhang** keep the discount vs history partly justified.
